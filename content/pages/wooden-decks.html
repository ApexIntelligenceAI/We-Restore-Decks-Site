FAQ

Questions homeowners ask about wooden decks.

What wood decking options do you offer?

We work with pressure-treated pine for practical builds and remodels, plus premium hardwood options like IPE for homeowners who want exceptional grain and durability. Railing options range from basic pressure-treated pine to high-end cable and metal systems.

Is wood cheaper than composite?

Generally yes on upfront cost. Wood offers a natural look at a lower initial price, while composite costs more but needs far less ongoing staining and upkeep. We help you compare both for your budget and lifestyle.

How often does a wood deck need staining?

Most wood decks in our region benefit from cleaning and re-staining every two to three years, depending on sun exposure, moisture, and foot traffic. We provide stain color guidance and care recommendations at project completion.

Can you replace just my wood deck boards?

Yes, when the framing is structurally sound. We remove old boards, inspect the frame, add flashing and framing where needed, and install new wood decking with a clean, updated surface.
