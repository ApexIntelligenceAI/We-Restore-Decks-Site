Oil Stain Colors

Armstrong Clark oil stains are wonderful on clear and clean wood. If your deck is new and has never been stained before, this might be a wonderful product for you.

Solid Colors

If your deck has had multiple coats of semi-transparent stain, or has been painted before with solid stain, then this is the category for you! These Olympic Elite solid deck stains have a smooth and durable finish. On average, these stains will last 3.5-5 years on flat surfaces and 5-10 years on vertical surfaces.

Semi Transparent

Think of semi-transparent stains that are NOT oil based as watered-down solid stain or paint. This means that the product can soak into the wood as it cures allowing the grain of the wood to shine thru. On average you'll see 2-4 years of quality look on the floor of your pressure treated pine deck and 3-5 years on the railings and other vertical surfaces.
