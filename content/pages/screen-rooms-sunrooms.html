FAQ

Questions Homeowners Ask About Screen Rooms

Can you remodel my existing screen room?

Yes. We can update screens, framing, and flooring, or convert a screen room into a more finished sunroom.

Can a new screen room be built on my current deck?

Often yes, if the deck's structure can support it. We inspect the framing and footings and reinforce them where needed.

What screen systems do you offer?

We install standard fixed screens as well as flexible systems like EZE-Breeze that let you switch between screen and glass panels.

Do I need permits for this type of project?

Usually yes. We help determine the permits required based on your location and the scope of the build.
