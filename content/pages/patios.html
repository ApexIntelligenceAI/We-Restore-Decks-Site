FAQ

Questions homeowners ask about patios.

What patio materials do you install?

We install paver, natural stone, and brick patio systems with options for borders, steps, seating walls, and fire pit surrounds. Material choice depends on budget, style, and how the space will be used.

How long does a patio installation take?

Most residential patio projects take one to two weeks depending on size, base conditions, and added features like fire pits, walls, or pergola connections. We provide a timeline after the site review.

Can you connect a patio to my existing deck?

Yes. We often design patio and deck combinations with coordinated levels, steps, and transitions so the entire outdoor area feels like one finished space.

Do patios need much maintenance?

Pavers and stone are lower maintenance than wood decks. Periodic cleaning, joint sand refresh, and optional sealing help the surface stay clean and stable through seasonal weather.
