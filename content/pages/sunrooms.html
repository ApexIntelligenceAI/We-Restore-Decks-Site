FAQ

Questions Homeowners Ask About Sunrooms

Can you remodel my existing sunroom?

Yes. We can update windows, framing, and flooring, or upgrade a basic sunroom into a more finished, comfortable space.

Can a new sunroom be built on my current deck?

Often yes, if the deck's structure can support it. We inspect the framing and footings and reinforce them where needed.

What are vertical 4-track windows used for?

They let you switch between open-air screens and glass, so the space stays comfortable across more seasons.

Do I need permits for this type of project?

Usually yes. We help determine the permits required based on your location and the scope of the build.
