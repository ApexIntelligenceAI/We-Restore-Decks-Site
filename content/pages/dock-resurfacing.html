FAQ

Questions homeowners ask about dock resurfacing.

How do I know when resurfacing is needed?

Splintering, cupping, loose boards, or a worn gray surface usually mean the decking is due for resurfacing.

Can resurfacing make an old dock look new again?

Yes. Replacing the walking surface with fresh boards dramatically improves appearance, safety, and comfort underfoot.

Do all old docks need full replacement?

No. If the substructure and pilings are solid, resurfacing the top is a cost-effective alternative to full replacement.

Can I get a quote before an on-site visit?

Yes. Share photos and measurements for an initial quote, then we confirm the details during an on-site evaluation.
