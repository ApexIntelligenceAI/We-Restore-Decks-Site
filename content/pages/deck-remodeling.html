FAQ

Questions homeowners ask about deck remodeling.

When is remodeling better than building new?

If the existing frame is structurally sound, remodeling reuses it and usually costs less than a full rebuild while still giving you new decking, railings, and finishes.

What parts are usually replaced in a remodel?

Commonly the deck boards, railings, stairs, and worn trim. Framing is inspected and reinforced or replaced only where it is actually needed.

Is composite always better than wood?

Not always. Composite is low-maintenance and durable, while wood offers a natural look at a lower upfront cost. We help you weigh both for your budget and style.

Can I get an estimate before an on-site visit?

Yes. Send photos and measurements for an initial range, then we confirm the final scope after an on-site inspection.
