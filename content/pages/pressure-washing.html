FAQ

Questions homeowners ask about pressure washing.

Do you use low-pressure washing on house siding?

Yes. We use a low-pressure detergent method for siding to clean effectively without the damage high pressure can cause.

What surfaces can you clean with high pressure?

Patios, driveways, walkways, pool surrounds, brick, stone, and other masonry hold up well to targeted high-pressure cleaning.

Can vinyl and composite decks be cleaned safely?

Yes. We use mild detergents and lower pressure to remove mold and grime without marring composite or vinyl surfaces.

Can I get an estimate before an on-site visit?

Yes. Share photos and surface details for an initial range, then we confirm the final scope on site.
