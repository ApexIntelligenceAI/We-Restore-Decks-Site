FAQ

Questions homeowners ask about composite decks.

How much does composite decking usually cost?

Replacing deck boards with quality composite on a structurally sound frame typically ranges from $30 to $40 per square foot, depending on complexity and the board line you choose. New builds and railing upgrades are quoted separately based on full scope.

Is composite always better than wood?

Not always. Composite is low-maintenance and durable, while wood offers a natural look at a lower upfront cost. We help you weigh both for your budget, style, and how much upkeep you want to handle.

Can composite decking go on my existing frame?

Yes, when the framing is structurally sound. We inspect joists, beams, posts, and connections first, then install composite boards and compatible railings on a frame that is worthy of the investment.

How do I clean and maintain a composite deck?

Composite decks need far less maintenance than wood. Routine cleaning with mild detergent and lower-pressure washing removes mold and grime without damaging the surface. We provide care guidance at project completion.
