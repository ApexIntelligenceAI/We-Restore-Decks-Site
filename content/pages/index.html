Frequently Asked Questions

Do you build new decks?
–

Yes. We provide custom deck building services for homeowners who want to add a new deck, expand an existing deck, or replace an outdated structure.

Do you install composite decks?
+

Yes. Composite decks are a popular option for homeowners who want a durable, low-maintenance alternative to traditional wood decking.

Can you repair an existing deck?
+

Yes. Our Deck Repair services can address damaged boards, railings, stairs, surface wear, structural concerns, and other issues affecting the safety or appearance of your deck. We also offer a free safety inspection and repair estimate for decks in need of attention.

Can you remodel my old deck?
+

Yes. A Deck Remodel can include changing the layout, expanding the deck, upgrading materials, installing new railings, improving stairs, and adding functional details such as built-in seating or lighting.

Do you build patios and patio covers?
+

Yes. We build patios and patio covers that create more usable and comfortable outdoor living space for relaxing, dining, and entertaining.

Do you offer screenrooms and sunrooms?
+

Yes. We design and build screenrooms, sunrooms, and Sunspace Sunrooms that create a more comfortable connection between your home and the outdoors.
