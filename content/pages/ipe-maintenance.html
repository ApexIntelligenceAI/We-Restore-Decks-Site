FAQ

Questions homeowners ask about IPE maintenance.

How often should I maintain an uncovered IPE deck?

Uncovered IPE usually benefits from cleaning and re-oiling once or twice a year to keep its rich color and protect the surface.

Why do pigmented oils matter on IPE?

Pigmented oils add UV protection that clear finishes lack, helping IPE hold its warm tone instead of fading to gray.

Can any contractor maintain IPE correctly?

IPE is dense and needs the right products and prep. Experience with hardwoods matters to avoid poor adhesion and uneven results.

Can I get an estimate before an on-site visit?
