FAQ

Questions homeowners ask about patio covers.

What types of patio covers do you build?

We build attached lean-to covers, freestanding pavilions, solid roof structures, and open slat pergolas in timber, cedar, and modern metal-and-wood combinations. Style depends on your home, budget, and how much weather protection you need.

Do patio covers require a permit in Maryland?

Most attached and freestanding structures over a certain size require a permit. We handle drawings, structural specs, and permit coordination as part of the project so the cover meets local code requirements.

Can you add fans and lighting to my cover?

Yes. We plan electrical rough-ins for ceiling fans, recessed spotlights, chandeliers, and outlet locations during the framing stage so everything integrates cleanly into the finished ceiling.

How long does a patio cover build take?

Most residential patio cover projects take one to three weeks depending on size, roof complexity, permitting, and added features like skylights or outdoor kitchen tie-ins. We provide a timeline after the site review.
