FAQ

Questions homeowners ask about new deck builds.

When is a new build better than remodeling?

A new build makes sense when framing is rotted or unsafe, footings have failed, or you want a layout that the existing deck cannot support. High-end railing systems often work better on a fresh footprint too.

Can you build a new deck where an old one existed?

Yes. We remove the old structure, evaluate the footings, and build a new deck designed for your space and needs.

How much more does a new deck cost versus a remodel?

On average, a new build runs about 35%–50% more than a remodel because of additional planning, demolition, footing work, and logistics. We only recommend a new build when it delivers the best long-term value for your situation.

Can I get an estimate before an on-site visit?

Yes. Share your ideas, photos, and rough dimensions for an initial range, then we finalize scope and pricing after an on-site visit.
