FAQ

Questions homeowners ask about outdoor decks.

How do I choose between wood and composite decking?

Wood offers a natural look at a lower upfront cost; composite costs more initially but needs far less upkeep. We help match the choice to your budget and lifestyle.

Can you build a new deck where an old one existed?

Yes. We remove the old structure, evaluate the footings, and build a new deck designed for your space and needs.

How long does an outdoor deck project usually take?

Most projects take from a few days to a couple of weeks, depending on size, materials, and design complexity.

Can I get an estimate before an on-site visit?

Yes. Share your ideas, photos, and rough dimensions for an initial range, then we finalize after an on-site visit.
