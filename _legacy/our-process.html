The W.R.D. process

Five Phases, One Clear Path

Our process is specific, thorough, and efficient so expectations stay clear at every stage — from your first message to the final review of completed work.

01

Phase one

Share Your Project Details

Tell us about your goals and the condition of your deck or outdoor space. Deck care is a major investment — we believe clients should receive clear expectations from the very first conversation.

  • Share photos, rough dimensions, and what you want to achieve
  • Receive an initial estimate range before the on-site visit
  • Confirm service availability for your Maryland location
02

Phase two

In-Person Evaluation

All estimates are performed in person with close attention to details that affect cost, scope, and final outcomes. We inspect thoroughly so recommendations are practical and accurate.

  • Honest evaluation of joists, beams, posts, ledger, and flashing
  • Review of existing materials and structural condition
  • On-site discussion of layout, access, and project goals
03

Phase three

Scope, Options, and Written Quote

We walk through material options, railing styles, stairs, lighting, and accessory choices — then confirm the complete scope in writing with timeline and payment terms.

  • Practical scope recommendations aligned with your goals
  • Clear total project cost with no surprises
  • Warranty coverage discussed during the estimate
04

Phase four

Scheduling and Production

Once scope and schedule are confirmed, our team executes efficiently with consistent site management and proactive communication throughout the build.

  • Organized scheduling and responsive project coordination
  • Quality-focused field execution and crew management
  • Smooth experience at every step of production
05

Phase five

Final Walkthrough and Care Guidance

We review every completion detail with you to ensure strong final results — and provide guidance so your deck or outdoor space performs well for years to come.

  • Thorough review of all completed work together
  • Care and maintenance guidance for long-term performance
  • Accountability and follow-through through the final handoff
