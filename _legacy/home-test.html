FAQ

Questions homeowners ask before deck repair.

How do I know if my deck needs repair or replacement?
–

If major framing remains stable, targeted repairs are usually possible. If core structure is compromised throughout, replacement may be safer. We confirm this during inspection.

What deck issues should be fixed first?
+

Loose railings, unstable stairs, weak posts, and damaged framing should be corrected before cosmetic upgrades because they directly affect safety.

Can I repair now and upgrade later?
+

Yes. Many homeowners use a phased approach: immediate structural and safety repairs now, then resurfacing or design upgrades in a later phase.

Can I get an estimate before an on-site visit?
+

Yes. Share measurements, photos, and concerns for an early estimate range, then we confirm final scope after inspection.
