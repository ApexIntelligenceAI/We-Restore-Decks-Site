Our Story

Why This Company Was Built

From a single conviction about doing better work to a team serving Maryland homeowners every day — this is how We Restore Decks came to be.

01

2012 — The Beginning

A Better Experience, From Estimate to Walkthrough

In 2012, Jonathan Shambarger started We Restore Decks after seeing too many homeowners receive promises that were not matched by production quality. He built this company to deliver a better experience from first estimate through final walkthrough.
